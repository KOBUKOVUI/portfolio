A LITTLE ABOUT ME

WHY I DO
WHAT I DO.

I chose information security not for the race, but for the questions that still need answers.

I am passionate about information security. Curiosity is what keeps me here: understanding how systems are built, how their components connect, and sometimes, how they can be broken.

I want to play a small part in making the Internet and the systems around it better, one step at a time. Finding a vulnerability is not the destination. The value lies in understanding the issue, sharing findings responsibly, and helping get them fixed.

It starts with curiosity and continues through contribution. Making systems safer matters more than rivalry or one-upmanship.
